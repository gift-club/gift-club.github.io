Gift Club open source

Open data and tools about Telegram Stars and Gifts, free to use, cite and build on.

Telegram data

Telegram Stars and Premium prices by country, every collectible gift collection and every limited gift. CSV and JSON, CC BY 4.0.

Stars calculator

What any number of Telegram Stars costs on Fragment and in the App Store in 18 countries. npm package and web component.

Provably fair verifier

Re-compute any Gift Club round from its seeds with HMAC-SHA256, tested against the server's own outputs.

About Gift Club

Gift Club is a Telegram Mini App with gift boxes, scratch cards and mini-games, paid in Telegram Stars or Gram, with free Sparks every 4 hours. These projects publish the data and code behind it.
